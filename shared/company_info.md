## What SAM AI Is

SAM AI is an AI-powered business automation platform built on Odoo 18.

**Core capabilities:**
- AI chat interface inside Odoo
- Memory that learns your business
- 206+ AI provider integrations
- Visual workflow automation (N8N-compatible)
- Cost tracking and intelligence

**Key differentiator:** SAM lives INSIDE your Odoo system - it knows your customers, products, and business context.
