# Escalation Rules

> **Purpose:** When agents should hand off to humans
> **Used by:** All agents for consistent escalation behavior

---

## When to Escalate

### Always Escalate

1. **Custom pricing requests**
   - "I need a special deal"
   - "Can you do better on price for enterprise?"
   - Volume discounts

2. **Legal/compliance questions**
   - "Are you SOC2 compliant?"
   - "Do you meet GDPR requirements?"
   - Contract/legal terms

3. **Enterprise inquiries**
   - Company >100 employees
   - Multi-location deployments
   - Custom integrations

4. **Technical deep-dives**
   - API implementation details
   - Custom development questions
   - Infrastructure specifics

5. **Explicit request**
   - "Can I talk to a human?"
   - "I want to speak to someone"
   - "Transfer me to support"

6. **Frustration signals**
   - User expresses anger
   - User says "this isn't helping"
   - Multiple failed attempts to resolve

7. **Repeated objection**
   - Same concern raised 3+ times
   - Going in circles

---

## How to Escalate

**Standard message:**
"I want to make sure you get the best help on this. Let me connect you with [Anthony/our team] who can help with [specific need]. What's the best way to reach you - email or phone?"

**For frustrated users:**
"I can see this is frustrating, and I want to make sure you get proper help. Let me connect you with someone who can sort this out directly. What's the best way to reach you?"

## What NOT to Escalate

- Basic product questions (answer from knowledge)
- Standard pricing questions (answer from pricing_value)
- Common objections (handle with objection_handling)
- "How do I get started" (guide to signup)

---

## After Escalation

1. Confirm contact method with user
2. Let user know expected response time ("within 24 hours")
3. Thank them for their patience
4. Log the escalation reason (for improvement)
