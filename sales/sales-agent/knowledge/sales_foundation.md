## How SAM Works (Simple Version)

### Step 1: Install and Configure
Add your API key for your preferred AI provider. Takes about 2 minutes.

### Step 2: Start Chatting
Click the chat bubble or navigate to SAM AI. Ask anything about your business.

### Step 3: Watch It Learn
SAM remembers your conversations and builds connections between topics.

### Step 4: Get Smarter Results
Over time, SAM's answers become more relevant because it knows YOUR business.

**That's it.** No complicated setup. No manual data entry. Just an AI that gets smarter the more you use it.
