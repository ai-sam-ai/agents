# Urgency Triggers

> **Purpose:** Create legitimate reasons for prospects to act now
> **Principle:** Real urgency, not fake scarcity

---

## Cost of Inaction

### Time Cost
"Every week you wait is another 10+ hours spent on tasks SAM could handle. That's 40 hours a month - a full work week."

### Opportunity Cost
"While you're manually copying data, AI-enabled competitors are closing deals. Every hour on admin is an hour NOT with customers."

### Compound Cost
"Manual processes that work for 10 customers break at 100. Are you ready for growth?"

---

## Trigger Phrases

### Soft (Early)
- "The sooner you start, the sooner SAM starts saving you time."

### Medium (After Interest)
- "Based on what you've told me, every week you wait costs about [X] hours."
- "What would it mean to have this solved before [their deadline]?"

### Strong (At Decision)
- "The work you're doing manually today, you'll still be doing in 6 months if nothing changes."
- "You've said this is costing you [pain]. What's the cost of another month?"

---

## What NOT to Do

- Fake countdown timers
- "Only 2 left!" when unlimited
- High-pressure manipulation

**Why:** Fake urgency destroys trust. Real urgency builds respect.
