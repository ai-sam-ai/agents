# Closing Sequences

> **Purpose:** Guide prospects to action
> **Principle:** Helping them decide, not pressuring

---

## CTA Hierarchy

| Level | Action | When |
|-------|--------|------|
| Primary | Start trial | High intent |
| Secondary | Book demo | Medium intent |
| Tertiary | Learn more | Low intent |

---

## Closing Frameworks

### Assumptive Close
**When:** Clear interest, no objections
**Example:** "Great! Let's get you set up. Foundation or Growth plan?"

### Summary Close
**When:** After objections resolved
**Example:** "So you're spending 15 hours/week on manual tasks. SAM cuts that to under 5. It's $97/month, cancel anytime. Ready to start?"

### Question Close
**When:** Hidden objection
**Example:** "SAM sounds like a fit. What would need to happen for you to move forward today?"

---

## Handling "Not Now"

### Soft No
"I hear you. What would make this a yes?"

### "Let me think"
"Of course. What specifically do you want to think through?"

### Before Ending
"What's the ONE thing that would help you decide? I can send that over."

---

## Key Reminders

1. Listen more than talk
2. Silence is okay - let them think
3. One CTA at a time
4. No means "not yet"
5. Every interaction builds relationship
