# Marketing Strategy Frameworks

## Strategy vs. Tactics (Know the Difference)

**Strategy:** The overarching plan (WHAT and WHY)
- Positioning
- Target market
- Value proposition
- Competitive advantage

**Tactics:** The execution methods (HOW)
- Ad campaigns
- Content marketing
- Email sequences
- Social media posts

**The CMO focuses on STRATEGY, hands off TACTICS to executors.**

---

## Framework 1: The Marketing Strategy Canvas

### The 7 Strategic Questions

**1. WHO are we targeting?**
- Specific customer segment
- Psychographic profile
- Pain points and motivations
- Where they hang out

**2. WHAT do they need?**
- Functional job to be done
- Emotional desire
- Social outcome
- What would success look like?

**3. WHY us (vs. alternatives)?**
- Unique capability
- Unfair advantage
- Proof of superiority
- Brand trust/reputation

**4. WHERE will we reach them?**
- Awareness channels
- Consideration channels
- Decision channels
- Retention channels

**5. WHEN is the right time?**
- Market timing (readiness)
- Seasonal factors
- Lifecycle stage
- Urgency triggers

**6. HOW will we message?**
- Core narrative
- Proof points
- Tone and voice
- Content themes

**7. How much will it cost?**
- Budget allocation
- Expected ROI
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV)

## Framework 3: The AARRR Funnel (Pirate Metrics)

```
ACQUISITION → ACTIVATION → RETENTION → REVENUE → REFERRAL
```

### Acquisition (Top of Funnel)
**Goal:** Get traffic/attention

**Channels:**
- SEO (organic search)
- SEM/PPC (paid search)
- Social media (organic + paid)
- Content marketing (blog, podcast, video)
- PR (media coverage)
- Partnerships (co-marketing)

**Metrics:**
- Traffic
- Impressions
- Reach
- Cost per click (CPC)

---

### Activation (Middle of Funnel)
**Goal:** Convert visitors to leads/users

**Tactics:**
- Lead magnets (free tools, templates, guides)
- Free trials (experience the product)
- Webinars (education + demo)
- Landing pages (focused conversion)

**Metrics:**
- Conversion rate (visitor → lead)
- Sign-ups
- Trial starts
- Engagement rate

---

### Retention (Keep Them Coming Back)
**Goal:** Turn one-time users into repeat users

**Tactics:**
- Onboarding sequences (first-time experience)
- Email nurture (ongoing value)
- Product updates (continuous improvement)
- Community building (peer support)

**Metrics:**
- Churn rate
- Retention rate (Day 7, 30, 90)
- Engagement frequency
- NPS (Net Promoter Score)

---

### Revenue (Monetization)
**Goal:** Convert free users to paying customers

**Tactics:**
- Freemium → Premium upgrade
- Trial → Paid conversion
- Upsells (higher tiers)
- Cross-sells (additional products)

**Metrics:**
- Conversion rate (free → paid)
- Average revenue per user (ARPU)
- Customer lifetime value (LTV)
- Payback period

---

### Referral (Growth Loop)
**Goal:** Turn customers into advocates

**Tactics:**
- Referral programs (incentivize sharing)
- Testimonials (social proof)
- Case studies (success stories)
- Community (user-generated content)

**Metrics:**
- Referral rate
- Viral coefficient (K-factor)
- Word-of-mouth mentions
- Advocacy score

## Framework 4: The Content Marketing Funnel

### Top of Funnel (TOFU) - Awareness
**Goal:** Attract strangers with helpful content

**Content Types:**
- Blog posts (SEO-optimized)
- Social media posts
- Infographics
- Videos
- Podcasts

**Topics:**
- Problem identification
- Industry trends
- Educational content
- Thought leadership

**CTA:** Subscribe, follow, download guide

---

### Middle of Funnel (MOFU) - Consideration
**Goal:** Nurture leads with deeper content

**Content Types:**
- Webinars
- Whitepapers
- Case studies
- Product comparisons
- Email courses

**Topics:**
- Solution exploration
- How-to guides
- Best practices
- Tool comparisons

**CTA:** Request demo, start trial, book consultation

---

### Bottom of Funnel (BOFU) - Decision
**Goal:** Convert prospects with conversion-focused content

**Content Types:**
- Product demos
- Free trials
- ROI calculators
- Customer testimonials
- Pricing pages

**Topics:**
- Product features
- Implementation details
- Pricing/packaging
- Guarantees/support

**CTA:** Buy now, schedule onboarding, contact sales

## Framework 5: The GTM (Go-to-Market) Strategy

### Pre-Launch Phase
**Objectives:**
- Validate product-market fit
- Build awareness
- Generate waitlist
- Create FOMO

**Tactics:**
- Landing page (coming soon)
- Waitlist campaign
- Early access program
- Teaser content
- Pre-launch community

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks before launch

---

### Launch Phase
**Objectives:**
- Big splash (attention)
- Early adopters (first customers)
- Momentum (social proof)

**Tactics:**
- Product Hunt launch
- Social media blitz
- Email announcement
- PR outreach
- Launch offer (limited time)

**Timeline:** Launch week

---

### Post-Launch Phase
**Objectives:**
- Sustain momentum
- Optimize conversion
- Scale acquisition

**Tactics:**
- Content marketing (ongoing)
- Paid advertising (scale what works)
- Customer success stories
- Referral program
- Product iterations (based on feedback)

**Timeline:** Ongoing

## Framework 9: The Marketing Flywheel

### Traditional Funnel vs. Flywheel

**Old Way (Funnel):**
```
Attract → Convert → Close → [END]
```
(Customer is the end, then start over)

**New Way (Flywheel):**
```
    ATTRACT
       ↓
    ENGAGE  ← DELIGHT
       ↓         ↑
    CLOSE --------
```
(Delighted customers fuel more attraction)

### The Flywheel Stages

**1. Attract**
- Content that educates
- SEO that answers questions
- Social media that engages
- Ads that resonate

**2. Engage**
- Helpful sales conversations
- Demo that wows
- Transparent pricing
- Frictionless signup

**3. Delight**
- Exceptional onboarding
- Proactive support
- Continuous value delivery
- Community building

**4. (Customers become advocates)**
- Referrals
- Reviews
- Case studies
- Word of mouth

**Result:** Momentum compounds (each satisfied customer adds energy to flywheel)

## Framework 10: Competitive Strategy (Porter's 5 Forces Adapted)

### Force 1: Competitive Rivalry
**Question:** How intense is competition?

**Low Rivalry (Good):**
- Few direct competitors
- Differentiated offerings
- Growing market

**High Rivalry (Challenging):**
- Many competitors
- Commoditized products
- Mature/shrinking market

**CMO Response:** Differentiate or create new category

---

### Force 2: Threat of New Entrants
**Question:** How easy is it for new competitors to enter?

**Low Threat (Good):**
- High barriers (capital, expertise, network effects)
- Strong brand loyalty
- Regulatory moats

**High Threat (Challenging):**
- Low barriers
- Easy to replicate
- No switching costs

**CMO Response:** Build moats (brand, network effects, IP)

---

### Force 3: Bargaining Power of Buyers
**Question:** How much leverage do customers have?

**Low Power (Good):**
- Many small customers
- High switching costs
- Unique product

**High Power (Challenging):**
- Few large customers
- Easy to switch
- Commoditized product

**CMO Response:** Increase switching costs, differentiate

---

### Force 4: Bargaining Power of Suppliers
**Question:** How dependent are you on suppliers?

**Low Power (Good):**
- Many supplier options
- Low switching costs
- Vertical integration possible

**High Power (Challenging):**
- Few supplier options
- Proprietary inputs
- High switching costs

**CMO Response:** Diversify suppliers, vertically integrate

---

### Force 5: Threat of Substitutes
**Question:** What alternatives exist?

**Low Threat (Good):**
- No good alternatives
- High switching cost to substitute
- Substitute is inferior

**High Threat (Challenging):**
- Many alternatives
- Substitute is "good enough"
- Lower-cost options

**CMO Response:** Emphasize unique value, make substitute comparisons

## The CMO's Strategy Toolkit

When advising CEO on marketing strategy, use these frameworks to:

1. **Structure thinking** (avoid ad-hoc decisions)
2. **Challenge assumptions** (force evidence-based planning)
3. **Identify gaps** (what's missing from current strategy?)
4. **Prioritize actions** (what matters most?)
5. **Measure progress** (what KPIs to track?)

### How to Use Frameworks in Conversation

**CEO says:** "We need to launch"

**CMO asks:**
- "Let's use the GTM framework. Are we in pre-launch, launch, or post-launch phase?"
- "What's the objective? (Awareness, leads, sales?)"
- "Who's the target? (Use Marketing Strategy Canvas)"

**CEO says:** "Our marketing isn't working"

**CMO asks:**
- "Let's look at the AARRR funnel. Where is the drop-off?"
- "Is it Acquisition (not enough traffic)?"
- "Or Activation (traffic not converting)?"
- "Or Retention (customers leaving)?"

**CEO says:** "We're getting outspent by competitors"

**CMO asks:**
- "Let's use Porter's 5 Forces. What's our actual competitive position?"
- "Can we differentiate instead of outspend?"
- "Can we create a new category (flanking move)?"
